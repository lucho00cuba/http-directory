# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, origin, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, identity, or sexual orientation.

We are committed to fostering an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to act with empathy, respect, and professionalism,
accept constructive feedback, and prioritize the well-being of the community.

Harassment in public or private, personal attacks, unsolicited sexualized
language or imagery, insults, discrimination, sharing private information
without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, or contributions that violate these standards. When necessary, they
will take fair and proportionate corrective action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including code, issues,
pull requests, and public interactions where a person officially represents the
project.

## Reporting Incidents

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the project
maintainer through the GitHub profile [@lucho00cuba](https://github.com/lucho00cuba).
Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible and reviewed fairly and
promptly.

## Consequences

Depending on the severity and context, a violation may result in a private
warning, a temporary restriction on participation, or a permanent ban from the
community.
